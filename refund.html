Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how you can cancel your edzhi subscription and our policy on refunds.

  • 1. General rules
  • edzhi subscriptions are billed annually in advance and renew automatically at the end of each annual billing period until you cancel.
  • You can cancel your subscription at any time.
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You will continue to have access until then.
  • To avoid renewal, cancel at least [30] days before the renewal date.
  • Subscription payments are non-refundable once the subscription has been activated.
  • 2. How to cancel
  • Email [Support@edzhi.com] from your registered email address with the subject "Cancel subscription", and include your Institute name and registered mobile number. We will confirm the cancellation in writing within [5-7] working days. Your plan will stay active until the end of your current billing period. [Optional: You can also cancel from Settings > Subscription & Billing in the edzhi admin panel.]
  • 3. Refunds
  • edzhi does not offer refunds on subscription payments.
  • Once a subscription has been activated, the payment is non-refundable. This applies to all Plans and all durations.
  • If you cancel mid-cycle, you keep access until the period ends. No pro-rated refund is given for the unused portion.
  • Promotional months, such as the "10 + 2 months free" offer, have no cash value and cannot be refunded or exchanged.
  • Messaging credits (SMS, email and WhatsApp), add-ons and custom Max Plan set-up fees are non-refundable.
  • Auto-renewals are not refunded if the subscription was not cancelled before the renewal date.
  • Exceptions. We will refund (a) a duplicate or mistaken payment, (b) an amount charged in error by us, and (c) prepaid fees if we end the Services for convenience under clause 15 of the Terms. Write to us within [15] days of the payment, and refunds go back to the original payment method within [7-10] working days.
  • 4. Free trial
  • Every Plan starts with a 10-day free trial. No payment is taken during the trial, and you can stop at any time. Please test the features carefully during the trial, as payments are non-refundable once the subscription is activated.
  • The free trial is optional. If you do not take the trial and start a paid subscription directly, or if you activate a paid subscription before the trial ends, the unused trial days will not be added to or adjusted in your paid period. Your paid period starts from the date the subscription is activated.
  • 5. Support
  • For any question about cancellations or billing, write to Support@edzhi.com with your Institute name and registered mobile number.
